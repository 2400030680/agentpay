Frequently Asked Questions

1. How do brand discounts work?

AgentPay collaborates with institutional merchants and Razorpay rails to unlock bulk corporate rates (up to 15% OFF). You pay the discounted amount and receive full face-value vouchers.

2. Where do I redeem my Voucher Code & PIN?

Copy the 16-character code and 4-digit PIN from your Voucher Orders. In the merchant app (like Myntra, Amazon, or PVR), open Gift Cards under Payments and paste the code + PIN to redeem the full value!

3. When do my vouchers expire?

All digital gift cards issued through AgentPay are valid for 1 full year (365 days) from the date of purchase.

4. What are Razorpay Coins?

Every transaction rewards you with coins (10% of saved rupees). These coins can be accumulated to redeem exclusive cashback coupons on future voucher orders.

5. Is my payment secure?

100%! All transactions are processed using official Razorpay 256-bit encrypted banking rails, compliant with RBI norms and ISO 27001 standards.

By authenticating with your mobile number, you agree to receive transaction-related OTPs and notifications. Accounts are tied to individual users and cannot be transferred.

Digital vouchers issued are non-refundable and cannot be exchanged for physical cash once the voucher code and security PIN have been generated and delivered.

All financial processing is handled strictly via Razorpay Software Private Limited. We do not store raw card credentials or bank passwords on our servers.

We adhere to the Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP) and RBI guidelines. User data is never sold or shared with unauthorized third-party advertisers.
